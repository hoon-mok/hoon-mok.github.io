Professor

Junghoon (Hoon) Mok, Ph.D.

Principal Investigator, CARE Lab · Kyonggi University

Prof. Mok studies the thermodynamics and kinetics of gas hydrates and their applications to CH4 recovery with CO2 sequestration, CO2 capture, transport and storage, and hydrate-based desalination.

Education

  • Ph.D., Urban and Environmental Engineering (Environmental Science and Engineering), Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST), 2023. Advisor: Prof. Yongwon Seo. Thesis
